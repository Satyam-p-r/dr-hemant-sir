Understanding the condition

Arthroscopy uses a small camera to inspect and treat selected knee problems. Individual symptoms and treatment needs vary.

Assessment and diagnosis

Symptoms, examination and imaging help identify ligament, meniscus or cartilage injuries.

Treatment options

Treatment may include rehabilitation, meniscus preservation or ligament surgery depending on findings. Arthroscopy is not routinely indicated for uncomplicated osteoarthritis.

Preparing for a consultation

Discuss the benefits, alternatives, risks and recovery expectations for your individual condition. Ask which facilities are appropriate for any proposed procedure.

Frequently asked question

Is every painful knee suitable for arthroscopy?

No. The cause of pain determines whether arthroscopy is useful.
