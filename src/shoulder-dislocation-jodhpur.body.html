Recurrent shoulder dislocation and Bankart repair in Jodhpur

Repeated shoulder dislocations may disrupt daily life, work and sport.

Understanding the condition

Repeated shoulder dislocations may disrupt daily life, work and sport. Individual symptoms and treatment needs vary.

Assessment and diagnosis

Assessment looks for labral injury, bone loss and other causes of instability.

Treatment options

Rehabilitation may help some patients. Bankart repair or other stabilisation procedures may be considered depending on bone loss and recurrence risk.

Preparing for a consultation

Discuss the benefits, alternatives, risks and recovery expectations for your individual condition. Ask which facilities are appropriate for any proposed procedure.

Frequently asked question

Is Bankart repair suitable for every dislocation?

No. Significant bone loss or other injuries can require a different approach.
