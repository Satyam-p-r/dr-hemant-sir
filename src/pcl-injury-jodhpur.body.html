PCL injury treatment in Jodhpur

The posterior cruciate ligament can be injured by a direct blow to a bent knee.

Understanding the condition

The posterior cruciate ligament can be injured by a direct blow to a bent knee. Individual symptoms and treatment needs vary.

Assessment and diagnosis

Examination evaluates backward translation and other ligament damage. Imaging may help identify combined injuries.

Treatment options

Bracing and guided rehabilitation may be appropriate for isolated injuries; surgery is considered for selected unstable or combined injuries.

Preparing for a consultation

Discuss the benefits, alternatives, risks and recovery expectations for your individual condition. Ask which facilities are appropriate for any proposed procedure.

Frequently asked question

Does a PCL tear always need surgery?

No. Injury severity, associated injuries and functional instability guide treatment.
