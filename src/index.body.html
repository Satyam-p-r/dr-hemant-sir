Know your options

Frequently asked questions

General information to help you prepare for your consultation.

What is arthroscopy?

Arthroscopy uses a small camera and instruments inserted through small incisions to examine or treat certain joint conditions.

Does every ACL tear need surgery?

No. Treatment depends on instability, activity level, associated injuries and clinical assessment.

Can repeated shoulder dislocations be treated?

Yes. Rehabilitation or surgical stabilisation may be considered depending on the cause and severity.

Are these procedures available in Jodhpur?

Arthroscopy and joint surgery are available locally. Consult the surgeon to assess suitability and facility requirements.
