Understanding the condition

Advanced knee arthritis may cause persistent pain and reduced mobility. Individual symptoms and treatment needs vary.

Assessment and diagnosis

Clinical assessment and weight-bearing imaging help determine the cause and extent of joint damage.

Treatment options

Exercise, medication and other non-operative care may help; replacement is an option for selected patients with severe symptoms.

Preparing for a consultation

Discuss the benefits, alternatives, risks and recovery expectations for your individual condition. Ask which facilities are appropriate for any proposed procedure.

Frequently asked question

Is knee replacement the first treatment for arthritis?

Usually not. Treatment is tailored to symptoms and response to non-operative care.
