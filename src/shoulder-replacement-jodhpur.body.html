Understanding the condition

Advanced shoulder joint damage can cause pain and loss of function. Individual symptoms and treatment needs vary.

Assessment and diagnosis

Imaging and assessment of rotator cuff function help determine suitable options.

Treatment options

Anatomic or reverse shoulder replacement may be considered for different patterns of joint and tendon damage.

Preparing for a consultation

Discuss the benefits, alternatives, risks and recovery expectations for your individual condition. Ask which facilities are appropriate for any proposed procedure.

Frequently asked question

What is reverse shoulder replacement?

A replacement design that changes joint geometry and may suit selected patients with deficient rotator cuff function.
