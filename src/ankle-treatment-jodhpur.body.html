Ankle injury treatment in Jodhpur

Ankle sprains and joint injuries can cause persistent pain or instability.

Understanding the condition

Ankle sprains and joint injuries can cause persistent pain or instability. Individual symptoms and treatment needs vary.

Assessment and diagnosis

Examination checks ligament stability, tenderness and ability to bear weight; imaging may be necessary.

Treatment options

Rehabilitation and support are common options. Persistent instability or selected joint injuries may require surgery.

Preparing for a consultation

Discuss the benefits, alternatives, risks and recovery expectations for your individual condition. Ask which facilities are appropriate for any proposed procedure.

Frequently asked question

When should an ankle injury be assessed urgently?

Severe pain, deformity, inability to bear weight or numbness warrants prompt assessment.
