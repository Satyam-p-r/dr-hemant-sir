Understanding the condition

An ACL injury can cause a knee to give way during turning, running or sport. Individual symptoms and treatment needs vary.

Assessment and diagnosis

A history of the injury, stability examination and sometimes MRI help distinguish an ACL tear from associated meniscus or cartilage damage.

Treatment options

Rehabilitation may suit some patients. Reconstruction is considered for persistent instability or particular activity goals; selected tears may be suitable for repair.

Preparing for a consultation

Discuss the benefits, alternatives, risks and recovery expectations for your individual condition. Ask which facilities are appropriate for any proposed procedure.

Frequently asked question

Is ACL repair the same as reconstruction?

No. Repair preserves selected native ligament tissue; reconstruction uses a graft. Suitability depends on the tear.
