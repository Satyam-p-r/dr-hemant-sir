Understanding the condition

Fractures require assessment of bone alignment and surrounding soft tissues. Individual symptoms and treatment needs vary.

Assessment and diagnosis

X-rays and sometimes additional imaging guide management; circulation and nerve function are important.

Treatment options

Immobilisation, reduction or fixation using plates, screws or other methods may be appropriate.

Preparing for a consultation

Discuss the benefits, alternatives, risks and recovery expectations for your individual condition. Ask which facilities are appropriate for any proposed procedure.

Frequently asked question

Can I wait for a scheduled consultation after major trauma?

No. Suspected open fractures, deformity or major trauma require urgent emergency care.
