MCL, LCL and complex knee ligament injuries in Jodhpur

Pain on either side of the knee after a twisting injury may involve collateral ligaments.

Understanding the condition

Pain on either side of the knee after a twisting injury may involve collateral ligaments. Individual symptoms and treatment needs vary.

Assessment and diagnosis

Assessment checks side-to-side stability and whether the ACL, PCL or other structures are involved.

Treatment options

Some collateral injuries heal with bracing and rehabilitation. Complex injuries may need repair or reconstruction. IT band tenodesis may be considered as an additional stabilising procedure in selected ACL cases.

Preparing for a consultation

Discuss the benefits, alternatives, risks and recovery expectations for your individual condition. Ask which facilities are appropriate for any proposed procedure.

Frequently asked question

What is IT band tenodesis?

A lateral extra-articular procedure using a strip of iliotibial band to improve rotational stability in selected knees.
