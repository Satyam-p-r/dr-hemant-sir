Meniscus injury and repair in Jodhpur

Understanding the condition

A meniscus tear may cause pain, swelling or catching after twisting the knee. Individual symptoms and treatment needs vary.

Assessment and diagnosis

The tear location, pattern, blood supply and associated ligament injuries influence options.

Treatment options

Treatment can include physiotherapy or selected arthroscopic repair. Preserving meniscus tissue is desirable when feasible.

Preparing for a consultation

Discuss the benefits, alternatives, risks and recovery expectations for your individual condition. Ask which facilities are appropriate for any proposed procedure.

Frequently asked question

Can every meniscus tear be repaired?

No. Some tears are not repairable and others do not require surgery.
