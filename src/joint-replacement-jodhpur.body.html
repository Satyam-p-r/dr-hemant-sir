Understanding the condition

Severe joint damage can limit walking, sleep and independence. Individual symptoms and treatment needs vary.

Assessment and diagnosis

Assessment considers pain, movement, imaging, general health and prior non-operative care.

Treatment options

Replacement may be considered when symptoms remain disabling despite appropriate treatment. Knee, hip and selected shoulder replacement options differ.

Preparing for a consultation

Discuss the benefits, alternatives, risks and recovery expectations for your individual condition. Ask which facilities are appropriate for any proposed procedure.

How long does recovery take?
