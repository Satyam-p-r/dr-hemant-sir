Rotator cuff injury and repair in Jodhpur

Rotator cuff tendon injury may cause weakness or pain when lifting the arm.

Understanding the condition

Rotator cuff tendon injury may cause weakness or pain when lifting the arm. Individual symptoms and treatment needs vary.

Assessment and diagnosis

Examination and ultrasound or MRI when appropriate help assess tendon integrity.

Treatment options

Options include activity adjustment, physiotherapy and selected surgical tendon repair. Rehabilitation after repair is gradual.

Preparing for a consultation

Discuss the benefits, alternatives, risks and recovery expectations for your individual condition. Ask which facilities are appropriate for any proposed procedure.

Frequently asked question

Does a tendon tear always need repair?

No. Symptoms, tear characteristics, age and functional needs influence the decision.
