Elbow injury and joint treatment in Jodhpur

Elbow trauma and stiffness can interfere with reaching, lifting and daily tasks.

Understanding the condition

Elbow trauma and stiffness can interfere with reaching, lifting and daily tasks. Individual symptoms and treatment needs vary.

Assessment and diagnosis

Assessment considers motion, stability, nerves and bone injury.

Treatment options

Treatment may include rehabilitation, fracture fixation or selected joint reconstruction or replacement.

Preparing for a consultation

Discuss the benefits, alternatives, risks and recovery expectations for your individual condition. Ask which facilities are appropriate for any proposed procedure.

Frequently asked question

Is elbow replacement common?

It is reserved for selected severe joint damage or complex injuries.
