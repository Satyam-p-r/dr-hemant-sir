Shoulder arthroscopy in Jodhpur

Shoulder arthroscopy may help treat selected tendon and instability problems.

Understanding the condition

Shoulder arthroscopy may help treat selected tendon and instability problems. Individual symptoms and treatment needs vary.

Assessment and diagnosis

Assessment considers shoulder movement, strength, previous dislocations and imaging.

Treatment options

Physiotherapy or surgery may be considered according to the diagnosis, including stabilisation or tendon repair.

Preparing for a consultation

Discuss the benefits, alternatives, risks and recovery expectations for your individual condition. Ask which facilities are appropriate for any proposed procedure.

Frequently asked question

Is shoulder arthroscopy a replacement operation?

No. Arthroscopy and joint replacement are distinct procedures.
